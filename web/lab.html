Features against the speaker's fingerprint

Each dot is one feature of this clip, placed by how many standard deviations it sits from the speaker's 20 enrollment clips. The shaded band is their typical range (±1 SD).

4

Weighted blend

Each component's distance is averaged with a fixed weight. Timbre, voice quality and pitch level are capped at 2 SD, so prosody decides the verdict.

5

Naturalness check

The contours are aligned with the other speakers' readings of the same sentence. A real person reads it within the normal human spread; a clone that phrases it oddly lands further out. With only two other readers per sentence this check is weak on its own (ROC-AUC about 0.67).
